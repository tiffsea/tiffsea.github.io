      <h2>🔍 Overview</h2>

      <p>This project explores the development of adaptive intelligent systems that combine machine learning, behavioral psychology, and robotics to support mental health and behavior change. With a background in both psychology and intelligent systems, I aim to design personalized, ethically-grounded AI agents for use in behavioral health, therapy support, and cognitive robotics.</p>

    </section>


    <section>

      <h2>🎯 Objectives</h2>

      <ul>

        <li>Model individual behavior patterns using reinforcement learning and behavioral profiling</li>

        <li>Create adaptive feedback systems that learn and respond to user states (e.g., mood, motivation)</li>

        <li>Build explainable AI systems based on principles from Applied Behavior Analysis and Health Psychology</li>

        <li>Evaluate prototypes through simulation and limited real-world interaction</li>

      </ul>

    </section>
